Overview

Why a ground platform

This project is in early design — extending the autonomous decision-making paradigm developed for UAV sensor deployment to a ground-based robotic platform. Where the UAV work excels at reaching elevated or hard-to-access points on infrastructure, a ground rover complements it with sustained, close-proximity inspection capability that aerial platforms can't match for flight-time or payload reasons.

The intent is for this rover to eventually share the same digital twin philosophy as the rest of the portfolio: physics-informed state estimation feeding autonomous or advised action — applied to ground-level infrastructure inspection and monitoring.
