UAV Autonomous Sensor Deployment for Infrastructure Monitoring

Teaching a hexacopter to see, localize, and dock on its own — closing the loop between perception and physical inspection of critical infrastructure.

Lab
ARTS Lab, USC
Stack
ROS · MAVLink · ArduPilot
Timeline

Role
Researcher
Overview

From manual flight to autonomous docking

This project tunes and calibrates control systems for a hexacopter UAV to achieve stable flight and precise maneuvering during docking operations — the precision step required before any sensor package can be reliably attached to civil infrastructure.

On top of that flight-stable platform, we're developing a deep learning model that replicates pilot actions through end-to-end control, enabling the UAV to autonomously identify, track, and navigate toward a docking station based purely on visual data — including in GPS-denied environments using stereo vision and fisheye ground-camera networks.

Methodology

Vision-based localization & approach planning

The UAV builds a real-time occupancy understanding of the structure it's approaching, fuses stereo and fisheye camera data for localization, and plans a path toward the docking point — all without relying on GPS.

Technical Approach

Stack & pipeline

Flight control runs through ArduPilot with MAVLink as the communication layer to ROS, where perception and decision nodes process incoming stereo and fisheye camera feeds.

03

End-to-end deep learning control replicates pilot docking behavior, reducing reliance on hand-tuned control laws.
