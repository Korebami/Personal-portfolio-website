Battery Degradation & Profitability Strategy for Electric Aircraft

Modeling how charging rate and flight routing shape battery life — then turning that physics into an operational decision strategy for profitability.

Lab
ARTS Lab, USC
Stack
MATLAB · Simscape
Timeline
Feb 2022 – Present
Aircraft Model
Electric Cessna 206
Overview

A multi-domain model grounded in real flight paths

This project developed and implemented a multi-domain model of an electric 6-seater Cessna 206 using MATLAB-Simscape, closely aligned with industry standards seen in the Pipistrel Velis Electro, to evaluate and optimize battery performance in electric aircraft.

Simulations analyze battery degradation across different flight routes, load profiles, and State of Charge (SOC) ranges using realistic Southeastern U.S. flight paths — not idealized test conditions. The goal from the start was extrapolation beyond observed data, not just fitting what's already been seen.

Key Finding

SOC range directly trades off against degradation

Evaluating specific SOC operating ranges showed up to a 3% reduction in battery degradation — a meaningful gain at fleet scale, but one that comes with a tradeoff in usable range and charging frequency that the profitability model accounts for.

Predictive Modeling

Remaining Useful Life estimation

Predictive models for Remaining Useful Life (RUL) incorporate thermal and mechanical stress variables, guiding sustainable and economical battery usage decisions — not just predicting failure, but informing what to do about it.

01

Thermal and mechanical stress integrated directly into RUL prediction, not treated as separate post-hoc corrections.

02

Ridge regression chosen deliberately for extrapolation beyond observed flight data — tree-based methods were ruled out for this reason.

03

Framework designed to scale across electrification efforts beyond aviation — applicable to broader sustainable transportation.

From Physics to Decisions

The profitability layer

The degradation model now feeds a decision strategy framework: given battery behavior as the dominant cost factor, which charging rates and flight distances maximize operational profitability over the aircraft's service life?

"Battery degradation isn't just a maintenance concern — it's the primary economic variable in electric aviation operations."

— Core thesis of the profitability framework
How This Connects

The template for the SMR framework

This lifecycle-modeling methodology — physics-based degradation prediction informing operational decisions — is the direct template applied to the SMR cyber-physical emulator. The aircraft proves the approach works; the reactor proves it generalizes.
