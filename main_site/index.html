Research

Four domains.
One unifying mission.

Each project explores how intelligent systems learn, adapt, and act in the physical world.

🛸

UAV Autonomous Control

Developing deep learning end-to-end control for hexacopter drones using ROS and MAVLink. Enables autonomous identification, tracking, and navigation toward docking stations for infrastructure sensor deployment — including GPS-denied environments using stereo vision and fisheye camera networks.

⚡

Electric Aircraft Battery Systems

Multi-domain MATLAB-Simscape modeling of electric aircraft battery behavior. Analyzes degradation across charging rates, SOC ranges, and flight routes. Builds predictive RUL frameworks and profitability decision models — informing which routes and charging strategies maximize operational life and revenue.

⚛️

SMR Lifecycle & Grid Interaction

Cyber-physical small modular reactor emulation for real-time control and grid interaction. Physics-informed lifecycle monitoring system that advises operational decisions based on expected component health — analogous to the aircraft battery framework but applied to nuclear energy systems.

🤖

Digital Twin Decision Systems

The convergence of all prior work: lifecycle-aware digital twins that close the loop between physical system behavior and autonomous decision-making. Currently extending into autonomous ground rover design and broader CPS integration frameworks.

The Common Thread

Physics meets intelligence — in every system.

Every project starts from the same question: what does a system need to know about its own physical state to make a better decision?

For electric aircraft, it's battery degradation driving route and charging strategy. For UAVs, it's real-time structural health data informing sensor deployment. For SMRs, it's component lifecycle state informing output and maintenance decisions.

The underlying architecture is always the same: physics-based modeling → lifecycle estimation → autonomous or advised action. That's the digital twin philosophy applied across domains.
