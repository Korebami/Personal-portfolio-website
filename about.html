Background

Bridging data-driven intelligence with real-world physical systems

I'm Korebami Adebajo, a PhD student in Mechanical Engineering at the University of South Carolina. My research sits at the intersection of physics-based modeling, machine learning, and autonomous systems — building frameworks where machines understand their own degradation and act accordingly.

I started as an undergraduate researcher in 2022 modeling electric aircraft battery behavior, and what began as a degradation study grew into something much larger: a unified methodology for lifecycle-aware decision-making that now spans electric aviation, UAV infrastructure inspection, and nuclear energy systems.

Outside the lab, I served as a Summer Research Program Ambassador and application reviewer at MIT, have tutored over 200 students at USC, and led the tractive systems team for an SAE Formula EV race car.
